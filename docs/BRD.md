## 1. Project Overview

Describe the project or feature, its business context, the problem or opportunity, and the intended outcome. Keep this section brief and avoid implementation details.

## 2. Objective

List the business outcomes the project should achieve. Write objectives as outcomes, not development tasks. Include a measure or target when one has been agreed.

**Example:**

- Reduce [business problem] by [target or measure].
- Enable [user group] to [desired outcome].
